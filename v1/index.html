04 / IN PRODUCTION

One case, told properly

Worth more than five vague ones. In daily use since 2025.

Sector
Hospitality
Client
Independent hotel · Aveiro
Problem
Manual invoices, slow monthly close
Status
In production since 2025

Hotel back-office

Dozens of supplier invoices a month were typed in by hand, checked by hand for duplicates, then cross-referenced against PMS revenue to assemble the P&L. Days of work for a number that arrived late.

Now the team photographs the invoice. The system reads the NIF, amounts and dates, recognises a duplicate even when it arrives through a different channel, classifies the supplier, and feeds a USALI-format P&L integrated with the PMS. The monthly close went from days to minutes.

  • Capture by photograph — no manual entry
  • Duplicate detection before payment
  • USALI P&L, generated in minutes
