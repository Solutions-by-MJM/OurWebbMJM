03 — In production

Hotel
back-office.

Independent hotel · Aveiro · Since 2025

Dozens of supplier invoices a month were typed in by hand, checked by hand for duplicates, then cross-referenced against PMS revenue to assemble the P&L. Days of work for a number that always arrived late.

Now the team photographs the invoice. The system reads the NIF, amounts and dates, catches a duplicate even when it arrives through another channel, classifies the supplier, and feeds a USALI-format P&L wired to the PMS. The monthly close went from days to minutes.

Proof before promises

Working software, not slides.
