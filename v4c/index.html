In production

Hotel back-office.

Dozens of supplier invoices a month were typed in by hand, checked by hand for duplicates, then cross-referenced against PMS revenue to assemble the P&L. Now the team photographs the invoice: the system reads the NIF, amounts and dates, catches a duplicate even through another channel, classifies the supplier, and feeds a USALI-format P&L wired to the PMS. The monthly close went from days to minutes.
